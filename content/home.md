strains_title: The strains
strains_content: We (Change Bio) received the YB-4290 strain of Komagataella phaffii from the ARS collection (NRRL) - we're calling this one PhaffiiNet-1 (PN-1) but it can also be called the type strain. We followed the steps given by Claes et al., 2024 for making their NCYC 2543 hoc1tr-1 strain. The genetic elements used are in the `sequences' folder and the workflow is represented in the OpenCloning file [YB-4290_hoc1tr.zip](YB-4290_hoc1tr.zip). To view this file visit [https://opencloning.org/](https://opencloning.org/), select File->Load cloning history from File, and upload the .zip archive. We've then called this strain PhaffiiNet-2 (PN-2).
